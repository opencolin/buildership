# BuilderShip · NVIDIA co-sponsor invitation

**Three-week remote AI hackathon · Finals on the bay · June 14, 2026 · San Francisco / Oakland**

Co-hosted by **Composio** and **Nebius**. Inviting **NVIDIA** to join as the third co-host.

→ **ship.builders**

---

## The event in one paragraph

40 hand-picked builders compete remotely from now → June 12. AI judges read every repo continuously, sponsor engineers run daily office hours, and the top 40 earn a boat day on June 14 — a chartered 89-foot yacht crosses the bay from South Beach to Jack London Square, all-day 1:1 conversations with sponsors + angels + VCs at our Oakland HQ, final presentations before sunset cruise, dinner at a waterfront restaurant, winners announced, and an after-party on the docked yacht. The grand prize: **$50K credits and a DGX Spark**.

## Why NVIDIA fits

- **Nemotron + NemoClaw showcase.** Builders are encouraged to use NVIDIA's open Nemotron models and the NemoClaw agent runtime as part of the sponsor stack. Every demo + repo + recap names the model and the runtime.
- **DGX Spark distribution moment.** The grand prize is a DGX Spark — given to the winner on the boat at sunset, with a photographer + videographer capturing it. NVIDIA hardware in the hands of the highest-leverage Bay Area builder, with content rights to redistribute the moment.
- **Nebius GPU pipeline.** All compute runs on NVIDIA GPUs hosted by Nebius — H100, H200, A100. Nemotron inference, fine-tuning workflows, and serverless deploys are part of the workshop track.
- **Bay Area developer reach.** 100+ expected applicants, all curated builders. AI Day / Future of Software Development / Nemotron talks (Bryan Catanzaro) already live in our workshop library. Builders see NVIDIA as an active partner.

## Audience + quality bar

- **40 builders, hand-picked.** Application is "show us your claws" — applicants post something they've already built publicly + tag the sponsors. We read everything and pick the strongest 40 of an expected 100+ applicants.
- **Bay Area-anchored** with builders flying in from elsewhere; the boat day is the only in-person requirement.
- **Judging rubric is public**: working demo, integration depth across the sponsor stack, usefulness, code quality, and pitch. AI judges (40%), sponsor engineers (40%), angels + VCs (20%).
