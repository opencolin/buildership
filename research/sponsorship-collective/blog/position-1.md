# **About the role…**

Our Closers work from home, full time, Monday to Friday 9AM - 5PM EST. This is an inbound role where you will have 4-6 meetings on your calendar per day. This is a demanding role and not appropriate as a “side hustle”.

You will be selling our Sponsorship Accelerator program (8500USD) to prospective clients. You will take charge of your own pipeline, booking follow ups, closing deals and fostering relationships. Each day will involve reaching out to prospective clients, getting clear on their challenges and pain and clearly communicating how their problems will be solved working with us. 

You will work alongside a committed sales team to achieve company objectives. We host a sales team huddle daily to train, learn and empower and share best practices with the team. 

 

## Key Responsibilities

  * Convert warm inbound leads into sales
  * Follow up with potential clients to maintain engagement
  * Build a pipeline of warm leads
  * Maintain an accurate and up to date CRM
  * Document conversations in the “notes” section of CRM
  * Take scheduled calls via Alloware and Hubspot
  * Attend daily sales huddles and any other trainings
  * Complete end of day reports and provide feedback
  * Consistently be on top of daily tasks and follow up calls
  * Exceed sales targets
  * Use a consultative sales approach

KPI’s:

  * 85K+ in new business monthly, which is the minimum KPI and represents 10 units
  * You should expect to take 4 – 6 inbound calls per day, 5 days per week
  * You will book all of your own follow ups until the deal is closed

Requirements**:**

  * 5+ years sales experience

Job Type:

  * Permanent Full Time (40 hours)
  * 1099 Contractor

Remuneration:

  * On target earning for this role range from $120,000 USD per year (minimum KPI) to $150,000 USD + per year
  * No cap on earnings

# **The Sponsorship Collective Core Values**

Our core values represent our North Star at The Sponsorship Collective. These values are what we use when creating processes, making decisions and evaluating progress. The right person for this role will appreciate and resonate with each value. 

EXTREME OWNERSHIP

Each member of the team does what they say they are going to do. Whether that is to hit our goals, ensure that our clients win or follow through on a promise, we do what we say no matter what it takes. We do not blame external factors for lack of success. 

SEEK FEEDBACK

We ask for coaching from our peers, clients, leadership and ourselves to shorten the feedback loop between testing and knowledge so that we can grow faster. 

TEAM FIRST

Everyone has a duty to hold their peers accountable. When someone is not up to our standard, whether colleague or client, we call attention to it and provide direct and honest feedback.

WORLD CLASS

We insist on the highest standards from ourselves, our colleagues and our clients. To give anything but our best is to let ourselves and our team down. We treat ourselves, our clients, our colleagues and our profession with respect by showing up as our best every day, even when we fail, have to deliver hard truths or challenge limiting beliefs.

As a closer you will embody these core values in every interaction at The Sponsorship Collective.
