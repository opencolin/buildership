## Sponsorship Has Changed.  
Have You?

Sponsors want strategic marketing partnerships that deliver results. But most sponsorship seekers are stuck in outdated tactics: gold-silver-bronze packages, vague pitches, and a whole lot of guesswork. Meanwhile, sponsors are drowning in proposals that don’t speak to their goals or their audience but instead are asking for donations.

That’s the gap Chris Baylis saw firsthand.

In 2007, he entered the sponsorship world after a career in tech and insurance sales — and quickly realized there was no playbook. No real training. Just trial and error. He built sponsorship programs, managed teams, and closed deals, but every lesson was hard-earned.

By 2014, fed up with the lack of guidance in the industry, Chris started sharing what he’d learned. His blog struck a nerve. Within a year, it grew into a thriving community, and by 2015, The Sponsorship Collective was born.

Today, Chris and the team lead one of the most trusted names in modern sponsorship strategy — helping people from all industries build scalable, sponsor-ready programs that get results.
