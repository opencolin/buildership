## THE MOST COMMON VALUATION MISTAKES

Basing your revenue goals on your costs

Thinking your audience is too small

Trying to sell small sponsorship because they are “easier” (they aren’t!)

Focusing on low value assets (logos, eblasts, signage)

Making up your value and your levels

Seeing sponsors as investors instead of marketers
