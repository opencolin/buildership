The afternoon

On the Thursday of SF Tech Week the Dragon Lady is out on San Francisco Bay from noon until five. The Blue Angels fly over the bay that afternoon. She will be underneath them.

There is Starlink aboard, should anyone need to know how Tech Week is getting on ashore.

  2. 2

  3. 3

    Move between decks, stay where the conversation is good, and look up when the jets come over.

Introductions

Before the day there is a short call with Smoov about what you are trying to get done and who would be useful to meet. Where there is a strong match, the group and the time are sent beforehand.

No one is rotated on a timer or seated at random.

Aboard

Founders, enterprise technical leaders, and the people who decide what gets bought. The sponsors’ founders are aboard too.

Fifty seats. The boat sets the cap.

The vessel

“Make it bulletproof”

Carmen Warschaw, to her naval architects.

As her captain tells it, she was standing directly behind Senator Robert F. Kennedy the night he was assassinated in Los Angeles. Afterwards she asked for a vessel that could get away, and come back. Asked where to, she said “Hawaii.” The fuel capacity was tripled.

Warschaw was a power in California’s Democratic Party for half a century. President Kennedy once opened a speech in Los Angeles by thanking “Carmen.”

Her adversaries called her the Dragon Lady. She took the name and gave it to her yachts.

This one was built in steel in 1979. Through the 1980s her salon received presidential candidates, governors, senators and ambassadors. Deals were made there, and campaigns quietly plotted.

She has been around the world. Today she is based at South Beach Harbor under Captain Jerry A. Deal.
